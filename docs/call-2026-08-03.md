# Handoff call — 3 August 2026

Bay Purcell (Search Visionary Tech) and Josh (Dynasty Marine Customs). 53 minutes.
This is the call this repository came out of. Recorded here so the agent has the context without
anyone having to re-explain it.

---

## What was decided

**Josh runs his own agent.** This is a handoff, not an ongoing service engagement. Bay:

> *"I don't want to fish for you. I'm handing you the pole. It's all you."*
> *"I'm not treating you like a client. I'm very much trying to help you set up your own system."*

Bay stays available for guidance, guardrails, errors, and a security review before anything
significant goes live — but Josh operates it day to day.

**Scope of agent access: everything.** Website, Google Ads, Google Analytics, GoHighLevel. Josh:

> *"Whatever we can automate. I need someone to be on top of marketing. If Claude can be monitoring
> everything."*

**Content becomes Josh's.** The site still uses build-phase copy and imagery. Bay offered to hand it
over rather than finish it, and Josh took that:

> Bay: *"If you want, I won't handle any of that and I'll be even quicker to hand it off to you."*
> Josh: *"That's honestly what I need to work on."*

Josh supplies photos and copy to his agent via Google Drive links.

## How Bay described the working model

On sessions and remote control:

> *"You can set it up so every session has a remote control feature. You can go on your laptop from
> wherever you are around the world, or your phone. You're literally interacting with your machine."*

On what to hand the agent:

> *"Any files you give it — a video, an image, link it to your Google Drive and drop the link — it
> can take all of that content, download it, and push it to the code for the website and publish it."*

> *"Any changes where you feel like you're not getting your point across with text: take a
> screenshot, draw a sketch on a piece of paper with a pencil and take a picture of it. It's very
> good at handling data and OCR and understanding what you give it."*

On the division of labor:

> *"It'll do all the heavy lifting for you. I would not recommend making it do all the decisions of
> the design work — you know your business better than anyone. You know what to showcase and what to
> promote. But all of these changes you want to do, just task it with it and give it the data it
> needs."*
